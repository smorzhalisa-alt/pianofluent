# Piano Scales — Gamified Badges

Companion badge system to the 13-level curriculum. Three tiers: consistency, progress, and quality.

---

## 1. Monthly commitment badges (resets each month)

| Badge | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 🔥 No Gaps | Practiced every single day this month — zero streak breaks |
| 🌱 Comeback | Missed a day but returned the next — rewards resilience, not just perfection |
| 📅 Weekly Regular | Hit at least 5 practice days in every week of the month |
| ⏱️ 20-Minute Club | Logged 20+ minutes of scale work, every session, all month |

## 2. Level badges (tied to the 13-level ladder)

| Badge | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 🎹 Level 1 Graduate | Completed all 5 steps (a–e) of Level 1 |
| 🏅 Sharp Side / Flat Side | Cleared all sharp-key levels (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) or all flat-key levels (3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13) separately |
| 👑 Circle Closer | Completed all 13 levels, including every Legendary step |
| 🌀 Enharmonic Twin | Cleared both Level 12 and 13 — the "same hands, new eyes" levels |

## 3. Skill / mastery badges (repeatable — can be re-earned at a harder tempo or new key)

| Badge | Criteria |
|---|---|
| ⚡ Fastest in the Room | Played a full 4-octave scale at a new personal-best tempo (track BPM per key) |
| 🗝️ All in Keys | Played all 12 physical keys' major scales back-to-back, no stopping (also doubles as the Level 13 capstone) |
| 🪞 Cross-Movement Master | Completed contrary-motion scale and arpeggio, in every level, with zero thumb-collision errors |
| 🎯 Steady Hands | Played a full scale with even volume across all notes — no accents on the thumb-crossing |
| 🌈 Chromatic Ninja | Nailed the Level 12 Legendary chromatic cross-movement, 4 octaves, no fingering rule broken |
| 🧩 Double Vision | Completed a scale in thirds or sixths without stopping to fix a hand |

### Bronze / Silver / Gold tiers for repeatable skill badges

| Badge | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⚡ Fastest in the Room | 80 BPM | 120 BPM | 160 BPM |
| 🪞 Cross-Movement Master | 3 levels clean | 8 levels clean | all 13 levels clean |
| 🎯 Steady Hands | 1 key even | half the keys even | all 12 keys even |

---

## 4. Badge logic

- **Monthly badges** reward *process* (showing up) — easiest to earn, build the habit.
- **Level badges** reward *progress* (moving through the curriculum) — earned once, permanent.
- **Skill badges** reward *quality* (how well, not just whether) — repeatable, re-earnable at harder tempos or new keys so they never go stale.
